Captains

Each captain sets their squad's pairs for Rounds 1 to 3 on their own player page. Once all four have submitted, the pairs go onto the scoreboard, phones and court sheets by themselves, and they lock: no changes after that.

How it works

  1. Open your link below, or scan the QR and find your name.
  2. Enter your mobile number. The first number you use becomes your key.
  3. Pick the wave 1 pair for each round and line from the dropdown (ratings shown); the other two fill wave 2. Submit by Friday, 6 pm.

Rounds 4 and 5: captains hand their lineup to the score desk on the day.

Game day

    
Saturday, Nov 7 at Park Padel SSF. Report 8:30, first ball 9:00. Keep this open on your phone.

    

      
    
        
  • R1–3 Round robin
  • R4 Seeding: 1st v 4th, 2nd v 3rd
  • R5 Finals: R4 winners for the Cup, R4 losers for third
    • 
      

      
    
        
  • Every round is two waves: 5 sets each, one per round. Every set is first to 6 games, golden point at deuce. At 5–5, a serve shootout: a racket toss decides who serves first; each player serves once. 2–2? The first server serves one sudden-death point. The winners take the set 6–5.
    • 
        
  • A tie is won on total games over its six sets. Round 4 or 5 tie level on games: more sets won wins. Sets 3–3 too: one golden point on all three courts at once, best of three.
    • 
        
  • Rank: total games, then game difference, then head-to-head games.
    • 
        
  • Scores: winners bring them to the score desk (Aanvi, Araya). Two taps there update the TV and every phone. Disputes: replay the point, then Akash or Rich.
    • 
      

      

      
Check-in: players scan the QR on the TV, find their name and enter their mobile number. The score desk sees who's in. No PIN anywhere.
